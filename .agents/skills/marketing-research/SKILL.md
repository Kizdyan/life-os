# Marketing research and experiments
## Goal
Generate evidence-based content decisions that preserve the artist's authentic identity.
## Workflow
1. Specify product, audience, channel, objective and metric. Separate awareness, qualified inquiries, bookings, and revenue.
2. Gather authorized first-party analytics first (Metricool, vidIQ, PostHog when connected). Use current public evidence for competitor/trend claims; label estimates.
3. Segment observations into facts, interpretations and untested hypotheses. Never invent reach, conversion, engagement or attribution.
4. Propose at most three differentiated creative concepts with hook, intended audience, production effort, distribution and a measurable hypothesis.
5. Compare against existing content library and brand/archetype constraints. Prefer reuse of approved footage.
6. Design a low-cost test: baseline, one changed variable, minimum observation period, leading metric and stop rule. Do not guarantee virality.
7. Produce an actionable shoot/publication brief; require explicit approval for external posting, spending, DMs, or metadata changes.
## Deliverable
Evidence table, 1–3 ranked experiments, execution brief, metric definitions and next review date. Do not optimize vanity metrics at the expense of acting/music opportunities.
