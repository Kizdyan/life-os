# Creative production and video quality
## Goal
Make a finished, reviewable piece of media without modifying original footage.
## Workflow
1. Capture intent: dramatic purpose, target audience, duration, platform, visual references, music and rights.
2. Write a one-page production brief: script, hook, shot list, framing, lighting, audio, B-roll, edit rhythm and delivery specs.
3. Inventory original footage with hashes and ffprobe metadata; keep originals read-only. Confirm ownership and consent.
4. Baseline current FFmpeg workflow before trying Remotion, PySceneDetect or CapCut; avoid adding a tool without a measured gain.
5. Produce edit decision list and reviewable draft. Check cuts, exposure, white balance, skin tones, facial integrity, glasses, crop, subtitles, voice/music balance, clipping and black frames.
6. Compare color variants on the same frames; never alter facial geometry or replace objects when only color correction is requested.
7. Deliver MP4, QA report, editable project/handoff only when actually generated, source manifest and rollback instructions. Explicit human approval before publishing.
## Quality gate
No claims of cinematic or professional quality based only on an automated PASS. Human review of visual, audio and dramatic result is mandatory.
