## Trust and release rules
- Personal finance: no stock tips or income promises from creator screenshots; finance forecasts are not investment recommendations.
- Personal behavior: avoid diagnosing anyone via entertainment quiz; consent and privacy for all personal data.
- Videos: don't copy proprietary footage, logos, audio, exact scripts, or other creators' identity; rights before publication.
- Research: retain source files and precise confidence notes, no generic autogenerated 'verified'.
- Code: this document is a curated backlog, not proof that each feature was deployed.
