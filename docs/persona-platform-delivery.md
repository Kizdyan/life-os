# Platform-specific delivery

Observed 26 November: Instagram accepted the PNG carousel while TikTok rejected image/png, requiring image/jpeg or image/webp. A planner status on a manual-notification job is not a platform publication receipt.

Editorial export preserves the original 1080×1350 PNGs and offers a separate TikTok JPEG ZIP from the same approved render. The JPEG conversion checks dimensions, MIME, byte signature and size; source and approval gates are unchanged. No external scheduling call is performed by this feature.

Delivery policy: separate destinations with different media requirements; a successful destination is not retried when another fails. Verify network status and platform permalink/ID where available. Manual notification is not proof of posting. For TikTok and Instagram photo content, music selection is now a required editorial gate. TikTok music must be selected and confirmed before publication; Instagram may be completed by adding music after posting only when the account demonstrably supports it. Do not choose random music automatically. A confirmed platform-specific permalink plus published status is required; manual notification alone is never proof. The policy helper distinguishes publication from music confirmation; no scheduler integration is implied. YouTube community posts, VK and Threads require a supported and authorized route; do not silently substitute a video or text-only post.

Remaining work: platform-specific automated outbox, idempotent retry and readback, caption adaptation, scheduling from measured activity. Validate real JPEG export on browser/runtime and one live TikTok delivery before accepting automation.

The TikTok JPEG ZIP includes delivery.json, declaring music AWAITING_NATIVE_SELECTION and publication NOT_SENT. The editor displays the native music requirement. personaDeliveryPolicy.ts and its tests describe receipt/retry/music rules; the external API outbox is not yet wired to these rules. The real JPEG retry succeeded on TikTok; the track is still missing. Next steps: per-destination soundtrack candidates and licensing/availability check, receipt import, authorized outbox, profile-level activity scheduling, cross-platform captions and daily editorial sprint.
