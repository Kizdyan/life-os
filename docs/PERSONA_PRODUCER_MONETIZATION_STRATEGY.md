# Persona — producer strategy and monetization brief (2026-10-27)

## North star
The Life OS ecosystem, with **DiveM leading strategy**, exists to grow **verified creative-professional income and opportunities** for an actor and musician, not to maximize posts or views in isolation.

Two parallel commercial funnels:
1. **Creator/brand**: discoverability → relevant audience → brand-fit evidence → inbound briefs → qualified paid collaboration → contracted revenue.
2. **Acting/music**: discoverability → credible craft portfolio → director/producer/casting/peer attention → professional conversation → audition or commissioned work → booked paid role / music commission.

Never report a view, like, follower, unqualified DM or speculative lead as revenue. Track leads, bookings and actual payments separately. No invented attribution.

## Audience segments
- Broad entertainment audience: humor, character, human stories, accessible everyday cinema.
- Film/TV/theatre decision makers: directors, casting, producers; proof of acting range, reliability, screen presence, performance excerpts.
- Creative collaborators: actors, filmmakers, editors, musicians; co-created formats and mutually useful portfolio work.
- Advertisers and agencies: brand-safe storytelling, on-camera skill, audience fit, production quality and delivery reliability.
- Music commissioners: composers, theatre/film teams, arrangers; finished work, process and clear offer.

A post may serve multiple segments but must declare one **primary audience** and one **primary objective** before production.

## Editorial pillars and visual formats
Pillars (rotate, do not lock fixed percentages):
1. Actor: mini-scenes, sketches, audition fragments, character work, backstage, actor craft.
2. Human/personality: visual diaries, getting-ready, routines, humor, relationship observations without exploiting private people.
3. Music: piano performance, arrangement breakdown, composition, theatre/film scoring, musical experiments, musical storytelling.
4. Collaboration: duets, ensemble sketches, creator/filmmaker projects, behind-the-scenes partnerships.
5. Trend adaptation: verified current trends, adapted to original character and brand; opt-in and rights-safe.
6. Commercial proof: polished brand-fit mock briefs (clearly labelled as speculative), portfolio clips, real collaborations with disclosure.

Visual grammars: direct-to-camera, voice-over+B-roll, cinematic observational, GRWM, process→result, sketch/mini-scene, multi-character scene, POV, montage, interview, photo-story, educational carousel, narrative carousel, portfolio carousel.

For each proposed idea deliver: why this audience cares; narrative tension/hook; format and reason; shot list with wide/medium/close/detail/action/reaction and pickup shots; voice-over/on-camera/live sound; music and rights; shot logistics (phone placement, light, duration, location); edit beat map and intentional acting pauses; caption/CTA; variants; measurable hypothesis; estimated effort and available source assets.

Do not substitute visual filler for narrative. Protect performance timing; do not force a cut every second. Batch shoot reusable detail shots without turning every post into a repetitive aesthetic montage.

## 90-day operating plan (rolling, not rigid quotas)
- Weekly **target**: 3 Reels, 1 personal-story photo, 1 carousel; Stories as appropriate; separate long-form lane. Do not publish low-quality content to meet quota.
- Keep 7–14 days of reviewed content in reserve. Batch by location/wardrobe/lighting, not only by platform.
- Week 1–2: baseline analytics, audience positioning, professional bio/contact/portfolio audit, shoot 2–3 visual grammars, verify full export QA and approvals.
- Week 3–4: repeat successful experiments, test first music and acting mini-scene series, develop outreach-ready showreel snippets and collaboration briefs.
- Month 2: compare audience and qualified-contact response by pillar; build creator/brand media kit with *real* metrics; test targeted collaboration and commercial offers.
- Month 3: scale evidence-backed series, pitch appropriate brands/collaborators/casting contacts, optimize the two commercial funnels and review actual earnings.

## Decision model (hypothesis-driven; DiveM owns ranking and targeting)
Every idea has:
- objective: reach | follower_conversion | qualified_industry_attention | collaboration | brand_inquiry | music_inquiry | portfolio_proof;
- audience: one primary segment, optional secondary;
- pillar + visual grammar + platform variants;
- evidence: source URL, observed date, sample/limitations for trends, rights/permissions;
- expected value: qualitative hypothesis, not invented numerical uplift;
- effort/cost, required locations/people, production risk;
- approval state and measurable post-publication outcome.

Balance exploration vs exploitation. Initial content-mix ratios are hypotheses; revise based on real results. No fabricated trend popularity. Trend research must cite current examples and dates; do not claim real-time trends from static templates.

## Scorecard (weekly and monthly; DiveM owns commercial measurement)
Awareness: reach, unique viewers, non-follower reach, qualified profile visits.
Engagement quality: average watch time, retention curve, shares, saves, meaningful comments.
Conversion: follows per relevant profile visit, portfolio clicks, inbound qualified messages, collaboration invitations, casting inquiries, music briefs, brand inquiries.
Business: qualified leads, meetings, auditions, booked jobs, contract value, cash received, repeat clients, source attribution when known.
Production: shoot/edit hours, render QA pass rate, revision count, approved buffer days, cost per approved asset.

Use platform APIs only where officially permitted and connected. Distinguish observed, user-reported and inferred metrics. Store denominator and observation window. Do not rank an industry lead by follower count.

## Commercial readiness (DiveM owner)
- Public professional positioning and clear contact method.
- Actor portfolio/showreel, credits and relevant professional materials, subject to permission.
- Music reel with rights-cleared excerpts and commission examples.
- Media kit: audience demographics, reach and examples based on verified numbers; never invent audience size.
- Rate card and licensing terms are negotiable, not invented market guarantees.
- Paid collaborations: explicit disclosure, permissions, usage rights, music clearance and platform rules.
- Personal relationships and private behind-the-scenes material require consent.

## Production system requirements
1. Research/reference intake → evidence-backed idea bank → producer ranking.
2. Select objective, audience and format → multiple distinct creative treatments.
3. Approved shooting brief → user shoots → ingest and deduplicate actual files.
4. Edit variants with voice-over, B-roll, music, acting-sensitive pacing and captions → technical and creative QA → author approval.
5. Platform adaptations and verified per-channel publication → analytics → learnings and next 90-day plan.
6. Record decisions, provenance, rejected concepts and reasons; never silently publish, change identity in images, or claim success without evidence.

## Implementation boundaries
This is the **product/producer specification**, not a claim that research agents, official publishing integrations, creator income attribution, trend discovery or real automatic montage already exist. The current branch contains a calendar, deterministic briefs, CLI timeline rendering and emerging QA. Integrate with canonical Life OS stores rather than duplicate tasks or media records. Do not merge or deploy before CI passes and user approval.
